# Code of conduct

Iyun is a place for learning, so please be decent to each other. Assume the other person means well. Argue about texts and code as much as you like, but keep the argument about the idea, not the person.

Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, reviews, or the study groups the software hosts. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove anything that crosses that line. They may ban people who keep doing it.

If something goes wrong, tell us. Open a private security advisory at https://github.com/Flipper1Zero/Iyun, or contact the repository owner directly.
