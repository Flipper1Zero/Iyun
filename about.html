About this site

Iyun (עיון, “close study”) is free, open-source software. It is an independent project. It is not made, endorsed or operated by any other study platform, and it uses only openly licensed texts, translations and fonts. The source code is licensed under the GPL-3.0; see the repository for the license and the full list of data credits.

What it offers

  • A library of more than a thousand texts in Hebrew, with an English translation where an openly licensed one exists: Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud (Bavli and Yerushalmi), Tosefta, Midrash, halacha, Kabbalah, Chasidut, Musar, Responsa, liturgy and commentaries.
  • The Vilna daf layout for all 37 Bavli tractates: Gemara with Rashi and Tosafot around it, chapter openers, search, bookmarks and notes.
  • A book reader with phrase-by-phrase translation, classic commentators beside the verse, find, citations, shareable links to a single verse, printing and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Study tools: markup (color-coding words), punctuation, flowcharts, audio sync, quiz cards, collections and notes.
  • Groups and sharing: private study groups with live messages and presence, following another member through a text, and public source sheets.
  • Hebrew and English interface, and it can be installed to your home screen and used offline for texts you have already opened.

What it is not

  • It does not offer school or classroom accounts, supervised student accounts, or institutional administration.
  • It is not a source of halachic rulings. For practical questions, ask a rabbi.
  • Translations, markup and machine-generated page layouts can contain errors. Check an edition you trust before relying on anything here.
  • About a thousand of the texts have no English at all, because no openly licensed translation exists in the data this site is built from. Titles that appear in other platforms’ menus but are missing from the open data are marked “not in open library” in the menu.

The name

Iyun is Hebrew for close, careful study: reading a text slowly, with its commentaries, until you understand it. The name and wordmark are this project’s own. Earlier versions carried a different working name; if you find a leftover, please open an issue.

Sources, licenses and credits

Open source and corrections

The code, the build scripts and the list of what is verified live in the repository. Spotted a mistake in a text, a mis-mapped title, or a broken page? Please open an issue. Errors in the underlying texts are best reported to the sources above.

Install it

On a phone or computer that supports it, use your browser’s “Install” or “Add to Home Screen”.
