THE READER

Read the text, not the interface.

Over a thousand open-licensed texts in Hebrew with a translation where one exists: Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud, Midrash, halacha and more. Step through a passage one phrase at a time, open the classic commentators beside the verse, and adjust size, vowels and theme from one panel. Every Bavli tractate opens in the traditional page layout with Rashi and Tosafot.

MARKUP

See how an argument is built.

Color-code words with a palette of your own and view the structure of a passage. Berakhot 2a carries a worked example of statement, proof, objection and answer; any other daf can be marked by hand.

GROUPS & SHARING

Study together, share what you make.

Private groups

Create a group and invite others with a code. Only members can see its messages.

Owner controls

The owner can rename the group, remove members, or delete it.

Live follow

A member can share their place in the text and everyone who chooses to follow moves with them.

Source sheets

Turn passages and notes into a public link anyone can read. Readers can report a sheet; its author can delete it.

Notes you keep

Notes live in your browser, work offline, and sync across devices when you sign in. Export them any time.

Installable

Add the site to your home screen. Texts you have opened keep working without a connection.
