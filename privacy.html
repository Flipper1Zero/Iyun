Privacy

Plain-language summary of what this independent study site stores. It describes how the software behaves; it is not legal advice.

Short version: reading needs no account. Your notes, bookmarks and settings stay in your browser unless you create an account, and then they are stored on the server so they sync between your devices. The site has no advertising and does not load third-party trackers or fonts.

Without an account

  • In your browser (localStorage): your notes, bookmarks, text marks, punctuation, preferences (theme, size, language), the last place you read, and the commentator you chose. Clear your site data to remove it.
  • Offline cache: pages and the texts you open are kept by a service worker so they work offline. Clear site data to remove them.
  • No cookies are set when you only read.
  • Audio files you load in Sync stay on your device; they are never uploaded.

With an account

If you sign up, the server stores:

  • your name, email address and a salted password hash (PBKDF2-SHA256, 100,000 iterations); the password itself is never stored;
  • a session: a random token in an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie for 30 days (only its hash is stored on the server);
  • your synced study data (notes, marks, bookmarks, tool data) as small key/value items, each up to 150 KB;
  • groups you create or join, their membership and the messages posted in them (visible to group members only);
  • source sheets you publish (public, see below) and reports made about sheets;
  • a short-lived count of recent sign-in attempts per IP address to slow down password guessing (rows are deleted after about a minute).

Live following and presence are delivered in real time and are not stored; whether you are online is visible to members of your groups while you have the site open.

Source sheets are public. Anyone with the link can read a published sheet and see the display name of its author. Do not put private information in a sheet. You can delete your sheets at any time.

Hosting and third parties

  • The site is served by Cloudflare. As with any host, Cloudflare processes request metadata such as IP address to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy. The operator may enable Cloudflare’s cookieless web analytics for the domain; it is not part of this site’s code.
  • This site loads no advertising, social widgets, analytics scripts or fonts from other companies. Links to other sites (Sefaria, Wikisource, GitHub) are ordinary links that you choose to follow.
  • If an optional AI question feature is switched on by the operator, the passage and your question are sent to the AI provider the operator configured to produce an answer. It is off unless signed in and configured.

Deleting your data

  • To remove everything stored in your browser, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • To remove your account and everything stored on the server (profile, synced data, group memberships, your messages, groups you own, your sheets), sign in and use the button below. This cannot be undone.
