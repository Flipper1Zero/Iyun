Review the drafts

Iyun marks the argument structure of the Talmud (Shakla v’Tarya) in six roles: statement, proof, attack, defense, question and answer. Almost all of it is an machine-assisted draft. It becomes trustworthy only when a person who knows the text checks it. This page shows how far along that is and how to help.

How to review a daf

  1. Pick a tractate below and open a draft daf. The reader shows the colored ranges and a “Draft” label.
  2. Read the Hebrew. For each range, check that it starts and ends in the right place and that the role (statement, proof, attack, defense, question, answer) and the short explanation are right. Correct anything wrong in the reader’s markup tools.
  3. When you are satisfied, fill in the form below to download a sign-off file, and send it to the maintainers (open a pull request that runs npm run annotations:signoff -- your-file.json, or attach the file to an issue).

Only sign off a daf you actually checked. A sign-off records your name and the date next to that daf in the public data.
